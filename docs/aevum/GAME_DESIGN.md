# Pokémon Aevum — Game Design Bible

## Core Identity

- Base: pokeemerald-expansion
- Pokémon scope: Generations 1–4
- Battle mechanics: Gen IV-style, including physical/special split
- Regional Dex: 200 regular catchable species
- Legendary/Mythical pool: 15 iconic species outside the 200
- Starters: Treecko, Torchic, Mudkip
- Professor: Professor Oak
- Rival: Zero
- Main antagonist: Dr. Julian Arden
- Founding Houses: Vale, Arden, Voss, Calder, Mercer

## Core Story

Ancient Aevum created Pokémon as military tools. Over time, Pokémon developed greater intelligence and independence, eventually rebelling against human control during an event known as the Severance. The resulting destruction collapsed ancient Aevum.

The surviving Five Houses created the Aevum Covenant and deliberately erased the truth to prevent humanity from rebuilding the Command System.

Modern Aevum discovers that truth during the player's journey.

The final story arc takes place in the Forgotten City beneath Mount Aevum and centers on Project ARCEUS.

## Core Gameplay Rules

- Party-wide EXP enabled
- Hard level caps
- Reusable TMs
- No Move Tutors
- Easy move relearning/deletion from any Pokémon Center PC
- HM-less field traversal
- No trade-required evolutions
- Custom logical learnsets for all 493 Gen I–IV Pokémon
- Faster walking/running
- Selective type redesigns
